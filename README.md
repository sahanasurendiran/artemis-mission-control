# 🚀 Artemis Mission Control

A NASA-inspired lunar mission simulator where players make decisions to guide Artemis safely to the Moon.

## 🌙 About

Artemis Mission Control is an interactive space mission simulator inspired by NASA's Artemis missions. Players take the role of mission control and make decisions during different stages of a lunar mission.

Each decision can affect the mission's **fuel, crew safety, and overall mission score**.

## 🎮 How to Play

1. Open the [live mission simulator](https://sahanasurendiran.github.io/artemis-mission-control/).
2. Click **Begin Mission**.
3. Read each mission challenge.
4. Choose the option you think is safest for the mission.
5. Continue through all four challenges.
6. See your final mission results.

## 🛰️ Mission Challenges

The simulator includes four major mission decisions:

* ☀️ Solar Storm
* 🧭 Trajectory Correction
* 🌕 Lunar Orbit
* 🚀 Lunar Landing
